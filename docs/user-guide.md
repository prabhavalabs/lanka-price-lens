# Using PriceLens

PriceLens shows what food costs today across Sri Lanka's open markets and supermarkets, how
prices are moving, and what a shopping list would cost at each store. It is at
[price.prabhavalabs.com](https://price.prabhavalabs.com) and works on a phone.

## The price board

The front page lists every product with a published price, grouped by category. Each card shows,
per seller group, the range across sellers in the unit most sellers use, how many sellers report
it, the day it was observed, and the change of the sellers' average over 30 days.

- **Open markets:** retail prices surveyed by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (Pettah, Narahenpita,
  Dambulla, Negombo) and the Department of Census and Statistics (Colombo district).
- **Supermarkets:** shelf prices from the online stores of Keells, Cargills, Glomark, and SPAR,
  captured every morning.
- **Wholesale markets:** the HARTI daily bulletin (Pettah, Dambulla, Peliyagoda, and others).

"Rising this month" and "Falling this month" pick out the biggest 30-day movers. Category chips
narrow the board; the search box in the header matches names in English, Sinhala, and Tamil, also
forgiving of spelling ("potatos", "b onion"), and after a pause adds products by the wording a
store or bulletin uses.

## A product's page

Open a card for the sellers by group, cheapest first with the cheapest marked, the supermarket
average against the wholesale average, and a history chart. Choose 30 days, 90 days, or a year,
switch seller groups on and off, and hover or tap a day to read every seller's price on it. The
range and groups are in the page address, so the view can be shared as a link.

A price older than its source allows (a week for a daily source, three weeks for a weekly one)
is shown struck through with an "outdated" badge and how long ago it was seen; it never counts
as the cheapest.

## Your basket

"Add" on a card or a product page puts the product in your basket, starting at half a kilo (or
one piece). The control then shows the amount: − and + step by a quarter kilo or one piece, and
tapping the amount opens presets (100 g to 5 kg, 250 ml to 2 l, 1 to 30 pieces) and a field for
an exact figure. Below 50 g or one piece the item leaves the basket.

The basket icon in the header opens a dropdown to adjust, remove, or clear items on the go.
"Compare stores for this basket" opens the basket page, which prices your list at every seller:
stores that carry the whole list first, then by total, with what each is missing, in the amounts
you set. Sellers whose newest price is older than 30 days are left out.

The basket lives in your browser only. Share it with the share button or WhatsApp.

## Cook with your basket

Below the store comparison, the basket page suggests dishes from a catalogue of 363 Sri Lankan
dishes, best fit first: how many of a dish's key ingredients you already have, how much of your
basket it uses, and what is still to buy. A dish page splits its ingredients into "from your
basket" and "still to buy" (today's cheapest price per unit, add in the amount you need), lists
pantry items, and gives a rough extra cost. "Recipes" in the header browses the whole catalogue
by name in any language or by ingredient.

## Theme, feedback, privacy

The theme follows your device; the toggle in the header sets light or dark and remembers it.
"Feedback" in the header or footer sends a note or a bug report with the page you were on. The
footer quietly shows how many people are on the site; nothing about you is stored beyond your
browser, and the site respects "do not track".
