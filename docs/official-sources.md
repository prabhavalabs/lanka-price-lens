# Official price publications

Besides the HARTI wholesale bulletin, two official PDF series are collected
through the same document pipeline (discover, archive the original, parse,
validate, score, promote). Each series has a **document adapter** that knows its
layout; everything else is shared. Manifests carry `"document_adapter"` and the
pipeline picks the parser from it.

| Source id | Adapter | Publisher | Cadence | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `harti_daily_food_prices` | `harti_daily` | HARTI | daily | Wholesale prices per market (the original source) |
| `cbsl_daily_price_report` | `cbsl_daily_price` | Central Bank of Sri Lanka | working days | Today's wholesale (Pettah, Dambulla, Peliyagoda, Negombo) and retail (Pettah, Dambulla, Narahenpita, Negombo) prices for selected food items and fish |
| `dcs_weekly_retail_prices` | `dcs_weekly_retail` | Department of Census and Statistics | weekly | Weekly average retail prices, price range, and week-on-week / year-on-year change for about 120 items across the main markets of the Colombo district |

## CBSL Daily Price Report

- Discovery reads the price-report listing page, which links each day's PDF
  (`price_report_<yyyymmdd>_e.pdf`). Older days sit on numbered pages; a backfill
  with `--from` walks back only as far as the range needs.
- Parsing finds the "Wholesale and Retail Prices: Selected Food Commodities" page,
  reads the group headings (Wholesale Prices / Retail Prices), the market headings,
  and the Yesterday/Today pairs, then keeps **Today** values only (yesterday is the
  previous report). Sections such as FISH re-declare their market headings and are
  handled the same way; a value column without a heading is skipped and reported
  as a warning.
- Every observation carries its own price type (`wholesale_observed` or
  `retail_observed`) and a market label such as "Pettah (retail)", so one document
  feeds both wholesale and retail markets. The wholesale Dambulla and Peliyagoda
  columns map to the same canonical markets as the HARTI bulletin.

## DCS Weekly Retail Prices

- The statistics site's listing page stops at 2017, but current reports live at a
  predictable URL (`/Resource/en/InflationAndPrices/retail/DCSB-WRP-<yyyy>-<mm>-W<n>.pdf`).
  Discovery probes the weeks in the requested range (default: the last ten weeks)
  with HEAD requests, falling back to GET, and keeps the ones that exist. A network
  failure for one week is logged and retried on the next run, never treated as
  "does not exist".
- Week *n* of a month is filed under its first day (1st, 8th, 15th, 22nd, 29th).
- Parsing reads every page's table: item, unit, last year's average, previous
  week's average, this week's average, two percentage changes, and the price range.
  Column positions are learnt from complete rows, so a missing value in one row
  does not shift the others. Bare qualifier rows ("Imported", "White", "No 2.")
  inherit the group of the row above ("Potatoes - Imported", "Egg - White").
- Observations use the price range as min/max (falling back to the average) and
  keep all averages and changes in the raw record.
- Full cream milk powder (400 g) is listed by brand under one heading; the brand
  rows (`Anchor`, `Maliban`, `Pelwatta`) map to the one canonical item, so the
  survey's milk powder price compares with the supermarkets' per-kilogram prices.

## Operating

- `foundry sync` now runs every enabled PDF source in turn (one failing source does
  not stop the others); `foundry sync --source <id>` runs one. The production timer
  uses the default.
- Mapping bundles are generated by `scripts/official-bundles.mjs` and reuse the
  canonical product and item ids of the HARTI and retailer bundles, so "Carrot" is
  the same item across wholesale bulletins, official retail reports, and
  supermarket snapshots.
- Fixtures under `foundry/test/fixtures/documents/` hold the extracted text of one
  real report per series; `foundry/test/documents.test.ts` covers discovery,
  parsing, and error codes.
